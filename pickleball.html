Pickleball Pirates @ Batts

Welcome to Pickleball Pirates @ Batts
We are a friendly group of pickleball players who meet several times a week at the Norman Booth Recreation Centre, Old Harlow. Whether you’re new to pickleball or an old hand, all standards of players are welcome to all our sessions. We also play competitively in the Hertfordshire Pickleball League. We are registered with Pickleball England Club 724
